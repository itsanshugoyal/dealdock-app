# 02 — Competitors and feature gaps

Research date: 31 October 2026. Scope: what DealDock lacks that would make Instagram-first commerce creators pay ₹4,000–₹15,000 / $49–$179 a month, inside the PRD's non-goals (no CRM pipelines or contact records, no auto-send, no bulk outbound, no contracts/invoicing/media kits, no content scheduling).

Method: web research on 2025–2026 tools, Meta developer documentation, and a read of `DealDock_Product_PRD.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `docs/private-pilot-readiness.md`, `docs/voice-and-audio-audit.md`, the Prisma schema, the Instagram Graph adapter and the creator-context repository.

Source caveat: most pricing and feature pages for DM-automation tools are written by competitors comparing themselves. Where a vendor's own page or Meta's documentation was available it is cited first. Prices change often. Treat every price below as "checked October 2026, re-verify before quoting externally".

## 0. Summary

1. **Sorting is free and automation is cheap.** Instagram ships inbox filters (unread, unanswered, followers, verified, story replies), custom folders, saved replies and a Partnership Messages folder at no cost ([Social Media Today, Aug 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-new-dm-message-management-options/758938/); [TechCrunch, Nov 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/01/instagram-upgrades-dms-with-focus-on-creators/)). Comment-to-DM tools cost $0–$40 a month. DealDock cannot charge ₹4,000+ for either, and should build neither.
2. **The bar for "drafts in my voice" moved in 2026.** Superhuman's Auto Drafts (July 2026) offers two alternates per draft and learns from what you send or reject. 60% of drafts sent within a day went out unedited ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/superhumans-new-auto-draft-feature-almost-makes-me-like-ai-replies/)). Gmail's personalized suggested replies became free in January 2026 ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/gmails-biggest-update-in-20-years-5-ai-features-that-could-change-email-forever)). Shortwave's Ghostwriter retrieves your own similar past emails as examples ([Shortwave](https://www.shortwave.com/blog/introducing-ghostwriter-ai-writing-that-learns-from-you/)). DealDock uses a single style card with no exemplar retrieval and no learning from edits ([voice audit](../voice-and-audio-audit.md)), so it is behind this bar today. "Generic wrapper" is a fair criticism of the current drafting.
3. **Beacons is the closest threat, and it is email-only.** Beacons launched an AI Brand-Deal Inbox (15 Jan 2026), per-deliverable rate suggestions (30 Apr 2026) and voice-matched Beam drafts learned from sent Gmail (18 Oct 2026) ([Beacons What's New](https://beacons.ai/i/whats-new)). It bundles a CRM, media kits and payments, which DealDock rules out. Nobody owns **Instagram DMs + coaching/course leads + value estimation + a ledger of creator-confirmed revenue**.
4. **AI "negotiator" agents auto-send.** Inbox Agent, CLOSR, Deals and Ginger reply to and negotiate with brands on the creator's behalf ([Inbox Agent](https://getinboxagent.com/), [CLOSR](https://www.closrcreator.com/), [Deals](https://branddeals.app/), [Ginger](https://gingerforcreators.com/)). DealDock's human-approval stance is a differentiator for creators whose reputation is the product. Market it as "your manager drafts, you decide".
5. **Native encroachment is real.** Meta Business Agent reached Instagram in June 2026. It does lead qualification, handoff and **morning briefings**, is free to start, and paid Meta One plans followed in September ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/meta-business-agent/); [DMCompared](https://dmcompared.com/explained/instagram-automation-trends/)). A daily briefing is now expected, not a differentiator. DealDock has to win on valuation, personal voice and proof of money.
6. **Platform finding that blocks a headline feature (P0).** Instagram allows replies only within **24 hours** of the person's last message. A separate **7-day Human Agent window** exists when the reply is tagged ([Meta, Instagram messaging API](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api); [Send API reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/reference/send-api%23limits)). DealDock's adapter sends `{recipient, message}` with no tag (`packages/messaging/src/instagram/instagram-graph-adapter.ts:223`). **Every default day-3 follow-up nudge, and every reply to a DM older than 24 hours, will fail with `SendWindowClosedError` in production.** That covers most of the "recover your biggest leak" flow. Fixing this is item #1 below.

## 4. Ranked top 12 additions for the redesign

Ranks 1–9 are **must-have** for a premium pilot; 10–12 are **nice-to-have** (11's phase 1 is borderline must-have for Hinglish-heavy cohorts). The repository touchpoints listed are where each change lands. They are not new layers.

### 1. Reply-window clock with the 7-day Human Agent window — must-have, S (code) + external review
Every thread shows when its reply window closes, counted from the counterparty's last inbound message (`Thread.lastInboundAt` already exists): a neutral chip for "replies open 18h", amber for "closes in 3h", and "Reply in Instagram" once 7 days have passed. The send gate attaches the Human Agent tag (`messaging_type`/`tag` per Meta's Send API) only when 24h < age ≤ 7d **and** a human seat approved that exact text, which the audit log already records. Nudge scheduling clamps to `lastInboundAt + 7d − safety margin`. When a creator's own slow reply leaves no room, the nudge row says so instead of offering a draft that cannot be sent. The push digest leads with "windows closing today". Per-class cadence (brand 2d, coaching 3d) answers the PRD's open question. Touchpoints: `instagram-graph-adapter.ts` `sendMessage` plus a typed `SendWindowClosedError`; `apps/worker/src/consumers/nudges.ts`; the thread header in the new UI. External work: request the Human Agent feature in Meta App Review and record the policy rationale (human approval per message). Without this, follow-ups (PRD §5.3) and most Leak Scan recovery fail in production.

### 2. Drafts that learn from the creator — must-have, L
This turns "style card" into a visible, improving voice. (a) **Exemplar retrieval:** each draft prompt gets 4–8 of the creator's own past replies, chosen by the same label, language and length band, the way Shortwave's Ghostwriter does it. (b) **Provenance:** samples are ranked native-Instagram-typed (echo with no `SendIntent`) > DealDock-sent unedited > DealDock-sent edited. Mostly-AI text is never used as the reference, which closes the audit's gap at `voice.ts:18`. Empty and non-text messages are filtered out. (c) **Edit learning:** for every sent draft, the stored `Draft.body` and `Draft.finalText` pair is diffed. A nightly job distills repeated edits into short **learned rules** ("You open with 'Heyy' not 'Hi'", "You never quote price in the first brand reply", "You switch to Hindi when they do"). Each rule shows its evidence count, and the creator can keep, edit or reject it in a "Your voice" screen. (d) **Weekly re-fit** of the style card from the newest approved sends, plus automatic first extraction after consent (both are gaps in the audit). (e) **Fidelity shown to the user:** "This week 74% of your drafts went out unedited (+9)". Everything stays per-IG-account and consent-gated, and revoking consent clears rules and exemplars. Touchpoints: `packages/db/src/repos/voice.ts`, `creator-context.ts`, `packages/ai/src/prompts.ts`, the drafting consumer, and a `styleCard.learnedRules` JSON (no new table needed at first).

### 3. Offers and rate card (private) — must-have, M
This is a short list of what the creator sells, per IG account: name, type (brand deliverable / coaching / course / other), price or range in org currency, a link (checkout, booking, intake form), availability ("2 slots left in Nov", "booking from Jan") and standing terms for brand work ("usage rights +30%", "exclusivity on request"). It is used three ways. Drafts quote real prices and links instead of inventing them, and the prompt forbids inventing any price not on the card. Value estimates are calibrated against it. Composer chips ("Insert coaching link", "Quote reel + story bundle") insert the facts on demand. This replaces the three single-price onboarding fields (`live-onboarding.tsx` sets min = max) and fixes the org-wide scoping gap for Roster. It is never public and has no media-kit export, so it stays within PRD §12.

### 4. Sender context card — must-have, S–M
At the top of the thread and in a compact form on inbox rows: avatar, name, follower count (locale-formatted), verified tick, "follows you" / "you follow them", "message request" (inferred from not following), first-seen date, and **history with you**: earlier threads, the last outcome, and ledger total ("Closed ₹1.2L with them in May"). Data comes from the User Profile API on first inbound plus a weekly refresh; the picture is stored rather than hot-linked because the URL expires. The same fields feed the classifier (verified + 50k followers + "budget" ≠ spam). The card is read-only and derived, with no editable notes, tags or stages, so it is not a contact record.

### 5. "Today" briefing, morning push and weekly digest — must-have, M
The home screen opens on a briefing ranked by money × urgency: "3 deals worth ₹4.2L need you · 2 follow-ups due · 1 reply window closes in 4h · 5 to review". One tap starts a triage run through them in order. The morning push (org timezone, after quiet hours) and a weekly email digest (V1 backlog; currently removed from Settings) carry the same numbers: recovered this week, replies sent, median reply time, value still open. Meta's own agent offers morning briefings, so this is the expected baseline. The DealDock version is denominated in money.

### 6. Close-out detection — must-have, M
The classifier pass flags "deal confirmed" signals in either direction: "confirmed", "sending the contract", "PO attached", "payment done", a UPI/invoice mention, "let's lock it", plus Hinglish equivalents. The thread then shows a gold banner, "Looks like this closed — log ₹40,000?", with the amount pre-filled from the conversation or the matching offer. A second sweep asks "Did this close?" for waiting threads silent for more than 14 days, with three answers: closed for ₹X / lost / still talking. This is one prompt and one ledger write, with no stages. It directly attacks the PRD's attribution-undercount risk (§10.1.5).

### 7. Draft steering: tone chips + alternates — must-have, S–M
Under the draft: **Shorter · Warmer · More formal · Hinglish/English · Ask for budget & brief · Quote my rate · Decline politely**, plus a free-text "tell it what to change". "Show 2 more" generates alternates lazily, only when asked, to control token cost; Superhuman's alternates are where users fix an over-eager default. Every chip and alternate choice is logged as a preference signal that feeds #2's learned rules ("You pick 'shorter' on 60% of coaching replies", which becomes the default). The draft stays a draft and nothing sends.

### 8. Speed-to-lead metrics — must-have, S
This shows median time-to-first-reply on high-value threads, the percentage answered within 4h and 24h, and "value answered late", each split by class. It appears as a small dashboard strip and in the digest ("You replied to brands in 3h 10m this week — top speed"). The data already exists (`lastInboundAt`, `Draft.sentAt`). It reinforces the PRD's 24-hour market reality and makes the product's effect visible beyond closed deals.

### 9. Keyboard triage, command palette and mobile gestures — must-have for the premium feel, S
Desktop keys: `j/k` move, `Enter` open, `e` approve & send (with confirm-on-hold), `w` waiting, `c` close for amount, `r` regenerate, `1–8` relabel in review, `?` shortcut sheet. `⌘K` palette: jump to @handle, "show follow-ups", "log a deal", "open ledger for May". Mobile: swipe right = waiting, swipe left = not-commerce/ignore, long-press = close. DESIGN.md §7 already promises j/k; this makes it real and visible. Managers on Studio and Roster tiers will use this all day.

### 10. Monthly money report, goal and recap card — nice-to-have, M
Month-end in-app report plus email: recovered (ledger), **leaked** (commerce threads unanswered for over 72h or past the 7-day window, at estimated value, labelled "estimate"), biggest win, fastest reply, fidelity. An optional monthly **goal** ("₹5L") shows as a ring on the dashboard. An optional **recap card** image is generated for sharing, private by default, with amounts that can be hidden and never any counterparty name. This is a word-of-mouth loop among creators. Estimates are always labelled as estimates, never as recovered revenue.

### 11. Voice notes end to end — phase 1 borderline must-have (M), phase 2 nice-to-have (L)
**Phase 1:** label inbound `audio` attachments correctly (the audit found them mislabelled as generic attachments), add an in-thread player, transcribe (Hinglish-capable), then classify and draft from the transcript. The row shows "Voice note · transcript" instead of "needs your ear". **Phase 2, opt-in and per creator:** after explicit voice-clone consent with a recorded sample, a reply draft can be rendered as a voice note in the creator's cloned voice (provider per the separate voice research; ElevenLabs as fallback). It is previewed and approved per message, sent as an `audio` attachment (m4a/wav ≤ 25 MB), and logged in the audit trail. Phase 2 is never allowed for nudges until it is proven, and it is disabled by revoking consent. This must stay within the PRD's "no bot pretending to be the creator" line: no real-time use, no autonomy, and a human approves each send.

### 12. Where it came from: source tags, collab link, ignore suggestions — nice-to-have, S
Tag each thread as **Story reply / Reel share / Ad / Collab link / Direct** from `reply_to.story`, attachment types and `referral.ref`, and show it as a filter chip and a classifier prior. Generate a personal **collab link** (`ig.me/m/<handle>?ref=collab`) for the bio or a pitch email. DMs arriving through it are pre-tagged as brand inquiries, and the click reopens the reply window. Suggest **ignore candidates** (frequent personal/fan senders the creator never replies to through DealDock) for one-tap ignore, which the PRD leans towards in §10.2.

## 5. Features we should explicitly NOT build

| Don't build | Why |
|---|---|
| Comment-to-DM, keyword or follow-gated auto-DMs, broadcast sequences | Auto-send and bulk outbound are both PRD non-goals. The category is commoditized at $0–$40 (ManyChat, Zorcha, CreatorFlow, SuperProfile, Stan) and Meta offers basics free. |
| AI auto-reply or AI negotiator ("closes deals while you sleep") | Violates never-auto-send. Meta labels automated AI replies, which hurts a creator's brand. Human approval is DealDock's trust position against Inbox Agent, CLOSR and Deals. |
| Pipelines, kanban stages, editable contact records, tags/segments | PRD "no generic CRM". Beacons, Inrō and Lnkk already do this. The waiting/closed states plus a derived sender card are enough. |
| Media kits, public rate pages, invoicing, contracts, payment collection | Explicit PRD non-goals. Beacons and Passionfroot bundle them, and competing there dilutes "money recovered from your inbox". The private offers card (#3) is the allowed version. |
| Outbound brand pitching or "find brands" | Bulk outbound is a strict no and a reputation risk. |
| Scheduled send | PRD §12 rules it out; Instagram schedules DMs natively. |
| Market rate benchmarks ("creators like you charge…") | PRD V3. It needs volume and consent across customers, and early numbers would be fabricated. Use the creator's own offers and ledger instead. |
| Real-time cloned-voice or persona chat; Meta creator-AI style bots | PRD bans bots pretending to be the creator. Voice notes (#11) are per-message approved drafts only. |
| Content ideas, post analytics, video tooling (Stanley, Edits) | Out of scope; Instagram Edits is free. |
| Chrome extension or Instagram overlay | PRD §12; policy risk. |
| Internal team comments on threads | PRD §12. |

## 6. Risks and open checks

- **Human Agent tag eligibility.** Confirm in the Meta App Dashboard that the Instagram-Login app can request it. Some third-party docs say it needs App Review and business verification. Keep a written rationale that each tagged send is human-approved, with the audit row as evidence. If it is denied, DealDock can only reply within 24h, and nudges must become "open in Instagram" reminders.
- **Shallow history** (20 messages per conversation) limits Leak Scan depth and cold-start voice. Item #2's reliance on ongoing sends and native echoes is deliberate.
- **Cost:** alternates and exemplar retrieval add tokens. Generate alternates lazily and cap exemplars at about 1.5k tokens.
- **Voice cloning** needs its own consent, storage, deletion and disclosure review before phase 2, plus verification that audio sent through the API renders as a playable voice note on real devices.
- **Competitor prices** in §2 are mostly from third-party pages. Re-verify before quoting them in marketing.
