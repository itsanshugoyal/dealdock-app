# Voice and audio audit

1 November 2026. This is a source review of the current local implementation, not a live creator-account, Meta, Claude, or speech-provider test. The wider findings and evidence are in the [authenticated product audit](ux-and-product-audit-2026-11-01.md). Demo data is fictional and does not prove provider behavior.

## Written voice

Voice is account-scoped application data in shared PostgreSQL tables: an organization and Instagram account identify a voice profile, its consent, style card, rules, samples, and related evidence. It is not a separately fine-tuned model or a separate database per creator. Business summary and onboarding price ranges remain organization-wide, so account-scoped voice and offers do not yet establish fully independent business knowledge for an agency. Repository and prompt context are in [creator-context.ts](../packages/db/src/repos/creator-context.ts), [voice.ts](../packages/db/src/repos/voice.ts), and [drafting.ts](../apps/api/src/routes/drafting.ts).

Explicit writing-voice consent is recorded per Instagram account. Granting it enqueues an initial refresh; onboarding also enqueues one, and a history scan schedules another after ingestion settles. Manual refresh is available, while a weekly worker schedules eligible consented, unpaused accounts. Refresh needs at least three usable samples and uses provenance-filtered outbound text to measure and describe style and seed rules. A manual refresh while learning is paused can update the card without seeding new rules. See [voice routes](../apps/api/src/routes/voice.ts), [onboarding](../apps/api/src/routes/onboarding.ts), and the [voice-learning worker](../apps/worker/src/consumers/voice-learning.ts).

Voice Studio exposes samples, exclusions, rules and overrides, pause, consent withdrawal, and a non-sendable preview. Draft generation retrieves consent-gated account context, including eligible examples and lessons, and records the profile version used. Human-approved sends record edits; an outbox drives deterministic evidence and rule learning, and, when configured, a batch of at least five edited sends may produce model-proposed evidence. Candidate and active rules have distinct states. Edit-rate statistics describe prior sent drafts; they are not a measured quality score for a new reply. See [Voice Studio](../apps/web/src/app/voice/page.tsx), [drafting](../apps/api/src/routes/drafting.ts), and [voice rules](../packages/db/src/repos/voice-rules.ts).

Consent, exclusions, and version checks fence in-flight work. Exclusions filter samples, edit batches, and retrieved lessons; profile-lock/version checks reject stale learning or draft writes after a relevant change. Withdrawing writing consent removes voice-derived artifacts and ready or edited drafts for that account. Excluding a single sample prevents its future use but does **not** retrospectively remove influence already distilled into a card or rule. A reliable provenance-aware rebuild/reset remains needed. See [voice repository](../packages/db/src/repos/voice.ts), [draft repository](../packages/db/src/repos/drafts.ts), and [worker learning](../apps/worker/src/consumers/voice-learning.ts).

The history pipeline only has the conversations and messages the provider actually supplies. Native outbound direction does not prove which person typed a message; an assistant or a prior tool may have written it. Source and edit provenance help selection but do not resolve that identity ambiguity. An organization role likewise does not establish that the person enrolling or authorizing a voice owns that particular creator account. Enforce that relationship before promising multi-creator voice enrollment. Instagram connection and writing-style consent are separate: the latter gates voice modeling and drafting, not all ingestion for the inbox. PostgreSQL row-level security is not established by these application-level checks.

## Incoming audio

Webhook and history adapters recognize audio attachments where the received payload supplies supported audio indicators. The ingestion worker attempts to download eligible media from an allowlisted Meta CDN with a size limit, stores it, and transcribes it when a speech provider is configured. Classification can use a transcript; without one, audio or another unreadable attachment goes to human review. The conversation UI includes a voice-note player and transcript when available. See [Instagram adapter](../packages/messaging/src/instagram/instagram-graph-adapter.ts), [webhooks](../apps/api/src/routes/webhooks.ts), [ingestion](../apps/worker/src/consumers/ingestion.ts), [classification](../apps/worker/src/consumers/classification.ts), and [conversation](../apps/web/src/components/conversation.tsx).

Actual attachment formats, historical coverage, expired or inaccessible media, transcription accuracy and recovery still require live provider/account validation. Detection code is not evidence that Meta supplies every voice note or that the feature works on a particular account.

## Speaking voice and outbound audio

Speaking voice has a separate consent flow: the in-app enrollment asks for a spoken consent take and additional recorded samples, validates the recordings, and calls a configured provider to create the voice. There are preview, settings and deletion paths. Speech adapters include ElevenLabs, a configurable self-hosted integration, and a development mock that production refuses. See [speaking routes](../apps/api/src/routes/speaking.ts), [recorder](../apps/web/src/components/voice-recorder.tsx), and [speech providers](../packages/speech/src/index.ts).

The authorized API send path can synthesize approved text and send an audio attachment; the worker does not independently send replies. When enabled, the synthetic-voice disclosure is a separate best-effort message, and a failure is returned as a hint rather than retrying the voice note. This is implemented code, not evidence of successful real delivery or audio quality. See [thread send route](../apps/api/src/routes/threads.ts) and [Instagram adapter](../packages/messaging/src/instagram/instagram-graph-adapter.ts).

**Keep customer audio sends gated.** The audit identifies outstanding controls: replacement can lose the vendor identifier when deletion fails; durable deletion work is needed; approval must bind to the exact previewed clip, text and voice revision; and duplicate synthesis must be prevented. The account-to-person consent relationship also remains unresolved. Validate real speech-provider deletion and quality, Meta media behavior, approved delivery, and disclosure failure handling before enabling the affected feature for a design partner. No provider test is claimed here.
