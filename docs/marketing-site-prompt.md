## What DealDock is

Only claim what's listed here. Items marked "check before launch" must be confirmed as live; otherwise say "coming soon" or leave them out.

DealDock turns Instagram DMs, and email, into a money inbox for people who sell through their messages.

- **Connection:** it connects through Meta's official Instagram API, with no scraping and no passwords.
- **Sorting and value:**
  - Every message is sorted by what it is: brand deal, coaching lead, course question, other sale, fan, spam, personal, or unsure.
  - Each gets a money estimate, and the biggest come first.
  - Unsure messages wait in "Check" for one tap.
- **Leak Scan:** on connecting, DealDock reads the last 30 days and shows what went unanswered, for example "₹3.4L in unanswered commerce DMs". Always label figures like this as examples.
- **Replies in the creator's voice:** every reply is drafted in the creator's own texting style, learned from their replies and edits. The rules it learned are visible and editable. It works in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
- **Approval:** nothing ever sends on its own. Every reply, follow-up and reminder is a draft that a person approves with a slide to send.
- **Follow-ups:** a nudge when a deal goes quiet, and a countdown on Instagram's 24-hour reply window.
- **From yes to paid:** when the other side says yes, DealDock:
  - fills the deal in from the chat: fee, deliverables, dates, usage rights, payment terms;
  - drafts a recap so both sides confirm the terms;
  - tracks deliverables and what's owed;
  - drafts a payment reminder when payment is late;
  - records the money in Revenue, including tax withheld (TDS).
- **Scam warnings:** it flags fake brand deals that ask for a fee, a login, or a locked file to open.
- **Email:** connect a Gmail inbox, so brand emails sit in the same inbox and get replies in the creator's voice. *Check before launch.*
- **Invoices:** GST invoices, LUT export invoices and a UPI payment QR. *In development: say "coming soon" or leave it out.*
- **Agencies (Roster plan):**
  - up to five creators, each with their own inbox, voice and revenue, managed from one desk;
  - creators can see everything sent on their account.
- **Voice notes (optional):** reply with a voice note in the creator's own voice, cloned only with their consent. Every voice note starts by saying it's AI-generated. *Check before launch; keep this a small mention.*

**Pricing, monthly (pilot):**

|---|---|---|---|

There's no setup fee and you can cancel anytime. The first invoice runs only after one full week of active use, and pilot members keep their price while they stay subscribed.

## Quality bar

- **Phone first:** right at 360–430px wide, never crowded, tap targets at least 44px.
- **Fast:** Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5s on 4G, no layout shift, fonts preloaded or self-hosted, images in modern formats.
- **Accessible:** WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled form fields, alt text.
- **Search and sharing:**
  - a unique title and description per page;
  - Open Graph images that show the product;
  - schema.org SoftwareApplication and FAQPage markup.
- **Form:** inline validation, spam protection and a clear success state. Never ask for passwords or payment details.
- **Analytics:** events for Apply clicks, form start and form submit (tool to confirm).
- **Meta brand rules:** naming Instagram to describe compatibility is fine. Don't use Instagram's logos as your own, and don't imply Meta endorses DealDock.
